Privacy

Saved items are stored locally on this device. In Local companion mode, conversations stay on-device in this demo build. No account is required.

About this demo

This build includes the complete Quran (114 surahs, Arabic with Saheeh International translation, bundled for offline reading), Qur’anic guidance and stories, 342 duas, and daily prayer times with city auto-detection, Qibla direction, a monthly timetable, and optional reminders while the app is open. Prayer times are calculated by Aladhan.com and should be confirmed with your local mosque. Production deployment should connect to authenticated Quran/Hadith datasets, a secure backend, and a retrieval-augmented generation pipeline with strict citation and safety controls.
